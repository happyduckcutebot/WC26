Build your World Cup bracket

Rank every group, choose the eight best third-place teams, then pick knockout winners. The app creates a one-page prediction sheet that you can print or save as PDF.

Start predictions

How it works

  • Drag-style ranking with up/down buttons for all 12 groups.
  • Top two teams advance automatically.
  • You choose exactly 8 of the 12 third-place teams.
  • For third-place bracket slots, the app suggests eligible teams and lets you adjust.
  • Pick winners through the final and print one clean page.
Step 1

Select final group rankings

Use the arrows to rank each group from 1st to 4th. The top two advance automatically; the 3rd-place team becomes eligible for the best-third selection.

Step 2

Select the eight best third-place teams

Choose exactly 8. The selected teams will become available for the third-place slots in the Round of 32.

Choose 8 third-place teams.
Step 3

Select knockout winners

For matches involving third-place teams, choose one of your selected third-place qualifiers in the dropdown. Then pick winners round by round.
